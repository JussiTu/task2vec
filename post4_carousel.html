Apache Hadoop  · n=83 engineers · complexity floor paradox
6–18× harder tickets.
Zero significant predictors.
Median complexity: Hadoop 0.18 · Spark 0.03 · Camel 0.01. Hadoop sits in a narrow high-complexity band — no easy tier, no gradient to grow across.
The complexity floor effect: when every ticket is hard, variance collapses and the rising arc becomes invisible. Hard environment ≠ growth environment. The environment must be designed, not just made difficult.

What to act on — already in your Jira & git logs
Two levers. No new tooling.
Both predictors require only standard Jira ticket data and git diff logs already stored by every engineering team.
01
Sub-task ratio
What fraction of this engineer's tickets are sub-tasks?
Above ~10%: scope ownership is fragmenting — they execute pieces, not problems.
Below 10%: growth conditions are present.
Spark p=0.019 · Growing engineers: half the sub-task rate
02
Scope delta — files/commit arc
Are they touching more files per commit this year than last?
Rising arc: scope expanding — cross-module, cross-cutting work.
Flat or falling: narrowing into comfortable territory.
Only Hadoop's founding engineers show positive file-complexity arc
Both signals are computable from data every engineering team already stores. No instrumentation. No surveys.
