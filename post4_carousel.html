The framework — widely adopted since the 1980s
The 70-20-10 Learning Model
How professionals develop skills, according to the standard L&D model
70%
On-the-job experience — doing real work, solving real problems
20%
Social learning — colleagues, mentors, peer feedback
10%
Formal training — courses, workshops, certifications
The model implies: give people the right work experience, and most of the learning takes care of itself. We wanted to verify the 70%.

The AI compounding effect
AI automates the work that
drives the 70%
1
The Assist tier — bounded, domain-specific problems — is where the most learning happens in our data. It is also where AI tools are most useful.
2
When AI drafts the solution, engineers review rather than solve. The experience is real but the cognitive load — the part that builds skill — is different.
3
70% of learning was already not happening. AI reduces the remaining signal further — in the tier that produces it most.
You can't compensate with training. The 10% formal learning component doesn't scale to replace the 70%. The experience is the curriculum.
