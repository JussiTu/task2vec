The specific risk with AI-assisted work
Two engineers. Same tickets. Different outcomes.
Scenario A — Learning happening
Engineer engages deeply → resolution time declines → Spearman rho negative → learning fingerprint visible.
Scenario B — Processing AI output
Engineer approves AI output at constant rate → flat line → velocity curve disappears → no learning signal.

The diagnostic — what to measure
The question isn't tickets closed per sprint.
It's whether they're getting faster.
↘
Declining resolution time within a ticket type Learning is probably happening. Worth encouraging.
→
Flat line across same ticket type Worth asking why. Could be quality AI assistance — or could be no engagement.
This works with Jira data you already have. Resolution dates + ticket categories. No additional tooling required.
